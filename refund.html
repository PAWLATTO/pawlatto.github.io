Refund Policy

PawLatto offers refunds under the following conditions:

  • Refund requests must be made within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused and in original packaging.
  • Custom or personalised products are non-refundable.

Refund Process

Once the returned item has been inspected and approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

Non-Refundable Items

Opened pet food, used grooming products, and custom items cannot be refunded.
